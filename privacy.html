PRIVACY POLICY · UPDATED OCTOBER 30, 2026

Your privacy matters.

This policy explains what information may be collected when you browse kissedbyevie-menu.onrender.com, create an account, contact me, or place an order. Please do not send sensitive information you do not want handled by the services involved.

Information you provide

If you create a free account, you provide an email address and login username. You may also provide a Snapchat, Telegram, or X username. Your 8 digit PIN is used for sign in and is handled by Supabase Auth. I do not ask for government ID through this website. The age entry confirmation is saved in your browser and is not an identity check.

If you contact me or place an order, you may choose to send usernames, order details, preferences, and payment or delivery coordination information in the chat or payment service you use. Do not send full card numbers, passwords, or account PINs in chat.

How information is used

  • To create and manage your account and sign in.
  • To remember the alert preferences you select and send the updates you request, if that feature is available.
  • To respond to questions, confirm orders, coordinate delivery, and provide purchased access.
  • To protect the site, prevent misuse, and keep basic business records.

Services that process information

The website is hosted by Render. Account authentication and related account data are handled through Supabase. The site also loads some fonts and scripts from Google Fonts and jsDelivr. If you follow a link or choose to pay or message through a third party, that provider handles information under its own policy. These services may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details, and the page requested.

The website does not process card payments or store card numbers. Payments and order conversations are arranged through the options shown on the site and the provider you choose. The menu may use your browser session storage to keep your current bag while you use the site. The age confirmation may be stored in local storage on your device.

Sharing and retention

I do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with the service providers needed to operate the site, account, communications, or payment method, or when required to protect rights, safety, or comply with law. I keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above, account operation, and legitimate recordkeeping. Third party providers may retain data under their own terms.

Your choices

You can choose not to create an account and browse the public menu. You can change alert preferences in your account when available. To ask about access, correction, or deletion of account information, message me on Telegram. I may need to verify that the request relates to your account. You can clear the age confirmation from your browser storage at any time.

Adults only

This site is intended for adults 18 and older. If you are under 18, leave the site and do not create an account or send personal information.

Policy updates

This policy may change as site features change. The updated date above will be revised when the policy is changed.
