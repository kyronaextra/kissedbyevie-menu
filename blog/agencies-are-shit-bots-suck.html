THE EVIE EDIT ✧ OPINION / TECH & CREATOR LIFE

Agencies are shit, Bots suck, can people stop downloading personalities??

By Evie·October 27, 2026·Unfiltered thoughts

About fake agencies, AI-written personalities, protecting client privacy, and why I'm building a creator tool that keeps the real person in the conversation.

Hey guys, so I need to rant for a second. And if you've been getting random DMs from sellers lately, this one's for you.

So here's the thing. I keep getting hit up by OnlyFans "agencies." And they all sound super legit. Like, "We're a team of five, working around the clock, 24/7, dedicated to your girl." Sounds great, right?

Yeah. It's a lie.

Because behind that "agency of five" is usually just one 25-year-old dude. Sitting in his mom's basement. In his underwear. Typing with one hand and eating cereal with the other.

That's the agency. That's the whole agency.

And what does this guy get out of it? Oh, everything. Free content from the model. A slice of her earnings. And here's the part that actually affects you: your data.

Because while you're out here thinking you're building a real relationship with a real person... he's copying your username. And every other guy's username. And then he's putting all of you on a list. A list of men who buy content. And he's passing that list around like it's a fantasy football league.

That's why I think random sellers keep finding you. You didn't get lucky. Your information may have been passed around.

And now? Now they're building AI models. And these things are good. Too good. They use lowercase letters. They've got grammar. They throw in a little humor. You're like, "Oh wow, she's so down to earth." Bro. She's a script. You can literally download "human-like personality" packs online and paste them into a bot that replies on its own.

These agencies go to models and pitch it like, "We're just taking the workload off you!" Some even offer to work for free. Free! And my concern is that when it's not cash changing hands, the real payment can be your information.

And I hate it. I'm an aspiring engineer. I'm literally in class right now learning about AI and cybersecurity, and AI is just torching this market for me personally. These bots are so cheap, clients genuinely can't tell the difference, and I can't charge for my actual time and labor because someone else is "doing it for cheaper." And why? Either they're ugly and need the sales... or it's just a bot doing the work in thirty seconds.

So that's the tea. If a random seller slides into your DMs out of nowhere, now you know one reason why. Your information might be circulating. Stay sharp.

I got tired of it, so I built something.

Now, here's where it gets fun, because I didn't just sit here and complain.

I've been locked in on school and my own life lately, which means my DMs have basically turned into a landfill. Time-wasters, AI-written messages from other creators pretending to be clients, spam bots. Opening my inbox feels like digging through a trash can to find one gold coin.

So I built a tool.

Yeah. I got tired of it, so I made something. It scans my messages and sorts them into categories: time wasters, honorable clients, low ballers, spam accounts, and sketchy models. That's five buckets. Five. And let me tell you, the "time waster" bucket is overflowing.

Next up? An automation that just blocks or deletes the unpleasant ones. No more reading "hey babe wyd" from someone unserious about purchasing. Gone. Deleted. Have a nice life.

Soon my chat list will be small enough that replying to the ones who matter is manageable again. And here's the part I care about: it's semi-autonomous on purpose. No bot replies. I'm not out here pretending to be a robot pretending to be a human pretending to be a girl. The real conversations? Those are still ME. I handle those myself.

It also shows me what's pending at a glance. Customs owed, FaceTimes waiting, live snaps requested. So instead of scrolling through 400 messages to figure out who's waiting on what, I just... look. Practical for me. Honestly useful for any creator who wants to stay different from the perfectly curated, artificial accounts.

Because let's be real. The bar is on the floor now. Half these "creators" are a spreadsheet with a personality pack.

Real engagement was always the point.

Here's the thing that gets me. Back in 2021, when people said "why would I pay, it's free online," my whole pitch was: I specialize in customized content and real engagement.

That was the whole point. And it's hard to keep the work meaningful when it's constantly getting devalued by spam behavior and men who want to be a woman so bad they're dressing up dolls online.

Which is funny, because the male peers who find out I'm a creator always tell me if they had a p*ssy, they'd be doing what I do. They dream of it, but at some points it ruined my perspective on my male counterparts, worse than it already was, and impacted my inner romantic relationships deeply.

I'm fine now. Choosing to be single.

So I'm building this. Not to automate myself out of the picture, but so I can actually focus on the clients who are still here. The ones who actually want engagement. The real ones.

Kill the noise. Focus on the people who matter. Simplee.

And about this site...

Right now, this is just a display and a menu reference. I'm focusing more on the back-ends and cloud stack so this can be really legit.

I aspire to work with it, fine-tune it, and make a system that works.

I will make the site interface installable for other creators too, and not give it to just anyone either. Real creators.

I'll probably even make it so that if it detects a false account trying to use it, it'll refuse to work on the site.

I am very passionate about ethical business practices when it comes to today's tech.

This is going to be great for my portfolio. I'm learning as I go, sometimes simultaneously working on my projects while listening to the topics assigned in my class.
